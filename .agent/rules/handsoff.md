2. WHEN GIVEN ANY `AUTOMONOUS` TASK ALWAYS CREATE A STEP BY STEP EXPLANATION OF WHAT YOU DID AND WHY YOU DID IT. THIS STRICTLY APPLIES TO ANY TASK THAT WILL BE EXPRESSLY LABELLED AS AUTONOMOUS.
